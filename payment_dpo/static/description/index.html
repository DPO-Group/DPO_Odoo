DPO Pay Payment Gateway

Accept DPO Pay payments in Odoo 17

DPO Pay Payment Gateway adds DPO Pay as a payment provider in Odoo 17. Customers are redirected to the secure DPO Pay payment page to pay, and are returned to Odoo once the payment is complete.

Features

  • Hosted DPO Pay payment page: card and mobile payments are captured by DPO, not by Odoo.
  • Configurable Pay URL: use the DPO live or sandbox payment page.
  • Service Type and Service Description from your DPO account are sent with every payment.
  • Customer details (name, address, city, country, email, phone and postal code) are included in the payment request.
  • Payment status is confirmed with DPO when the customer returns to Odoo.

Setup

  1. Install DPO Pay Payment Gateway.
  2. Go to Website or Invoicing → Configuration → Payment Providers → DPO Pay.
  3. Enter your Company Token, Service Type and Pay URL from DPO, choose Test Mode or Enabled and save.

Odoo 19 and later include their own DPO payment provider. Use DPO Pay Extras (payment_dpo_extras) there instead of this module.
